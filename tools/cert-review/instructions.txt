SEVERITY (Lars, 2026-10-14, the cert lane's rules, verbatim): roll forward, don't reject over minor issues. FAIL only for: a security or secret exposure, data loss or corruption, a supply-chain integrity problem (sha/provenance mismatch, an unexplained non-reproducible build), a license violation, a crash/boot failure, or a regression that breaks a common workflow without a workaround. Everything else (narrow regressions with workarounds, cosmetic issues, test gaps, docs) is a known issue: report it with a repro, and the line reads certified <pkg>@<v> sha256=<...> (known: <issue url>, ...).
